How QUE works

How it works

QUE shows one offer at a time instead of ranking or comparing brands. Pick a category and QUE displays a single offer drawn from that category's pool.

Randomization

Offers are selected randomly within the selected category. The first offer is randomized when the page loads. "Next offer →" picks another random offer and won't immediately repeat the one you're viewing. Randomness only means the order isn't ranked or curated — it doesn't guarantee equal exposure, fairness, quality, or suitability.

What you're seeing

Brand and provider names stay hidden until you choose to reveal them. That's a presentation choice meant to reduce brand-first bias, not an attempt to hide who's behind an offer permanently. The category you choose determines which pool of offers can appear.

Links & sources

"Learn more →" takes you directly to that offer's official pricing or product page. QUE's own "what you're getting" and "what it costs" summaries are informational paraphrases, not a provider's official wording.

Updates & accuracy

Pricing, features, limits, and availability change and can go out of date here. Always confirm details on the linked official page before deciding. Offers may be added, removed, or updated over time.

Commercial relationships

QUE does not sell, endorse, recommend, rank, or guarantee any offer shown, and isn't affiliated with any company represented here unless explicitly stated. If a sponsorship, affiliate link, paid placement, or other commercial relationship exists in the future, it will be disclosed in this panel.

Privacy

QUE is a static widget. It doesn't require an account and doesn't intentionally collect personal information.
