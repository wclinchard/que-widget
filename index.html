How QUE works

What QUE is

QUE is a brand-neutral discovery system — a way to see what you'd actually get and what it costs before you know whose it is. "Offer" isn't limited to deals or discounts: it covers products, services, subscriptions, and normal pricing plans — anything with a clear "here's what you get, here's what it costs" shape. Every offer gets the same even playing field: shown one at a time, on its own terms, instead of a ranked list or a wall of logos where brand recognition does the deciding.

How to use QUE

Pick a category from the row under "QUE" — that's the pool QUE will show offers from. "Next offer →" shows another random offer from that pool; "← Back" returns to whatever you were just looking at (your actual history, not another random pick). Both also work with the ArrowRight / ArrowLeft keys. The small eye icon reveals the brand: click once to arm it, click again to confirm, and it'll show the name briefly before hiding itself again. "Copy" grabs the offer's details as plain text, and "Share" copies a direct link to the offer you're looking at. "Learn more →" opens the provider's real site. If a list runs longer than usual, it scrolls within its own box — a small arrow next to the section title shows when there's more below. And if you think an offer belongs here, "Suggest an offer →" next to the QUE logo opens a separate submission form.

Sharing a link

The address bar always matches whatever offer is on screen, and "Share" copies that same link. Opening a shared link takes you straight to that exact offer instead of a random one. The link only encodes which offer it is (its category and an internal provider label) — nothing about you, and it doesn't create or require any kind of account.

Why the offer comes before the brand

A familiar logo can make the same offer feel more trustworthy, and an unfamiliar one can make it feel riskier, regardless of what's actually being offered. Hiding the provider name at first keeps that even playing field: "what you're getting" and "what it costs" get read on their own terms, before brand recognition can tip the scale. Nothing is permanently hidden — you can reveal the brand any time.

Categories

Offers are grouped into categories (AI, Design & Creative, Productivity, and so on). Choosing a category only limits which pool of offers can appear — it doesn't rank or prioritize anything within that pool. Categories with no offers yet simply don't show up in the filter. The left-to-right order of the category pills is also shuffled each time the page loads, so their position isn't a ranking either.

How offers are chosen

Within a category, QUE uses a "shuffle-bag": every offer is shown once, in a random order, before any offer repeats. Once the whole pool has been shown, it reshuffles into a new random order for the next cycle, and the last offer of one cycle is never immediately followed by itself in the next. This process doesn't rank, score, or prioritize any offer over another — it's random order, not a leaderboard.

Can payment affect placement?

No — QUE doesn't do pay-to-play placement. Which offer appears, and in what order, is decided only by the shuffle-bag described above; placement, ranking, and visibility are not for sale, and no offer can pay to appear more often or skip the shuffle. Where money does come in: QUE's current model charges providers per redirect — a click that sends someone to their site via "Learn more" — not a flat subscription or a fee tied to exposure. The current rate is $10 per 1,000 redirects, so cost tracks traffic actually delivered, not how often an offer is shown. Any future sponsorship, affiliate relationship, or other commercial arrangement will be disclosed in this panel before it affects anything you see, and a commercial relationship is never allowed to secretly influence which offer shows up or when.

Where offers come from

Anyone can submit an offer — use "Suggest an offer" next to the QUE logo. Submissions are currently curated and entered manually, based on each provider's own publicly available pricing and product details — there's no automated feed yet. Existing offers are reviewed and updated as pricing or details change, but on no fixed schedule, so the provider's own source is always the most current. See "Requests & corrections" below to flag one that's out of date.

What "have explored" means

Each offer shows how many times "Learn more" has been clicked for it — the same redirects QUE's usage-based costs are tied to (see "Can payment affect placement?"). This is browser-based tracking for the current MVP, not verified unique-person data or the actual billing system — it reflects clicks a browser has recorded, not confirmed individual people, and it resets like any other locally stored browser data if that storage is cleared.

Clicking "Learn more"

Clicking "Learn more" opens the provider's own official website in a new tab — that's the redirect — and, once per offer per browser, it counts toward that offer's exploration number. QUE isn't affiliated with, endorsed by, or representing any listed provider unless explicitly stated.

Accuracy & updates

Pricing, features, limits, and availability change, and the information shown here can fall out of date. Always confirm details on the provider's own site before deciding anything. The summaries on each card ("what you're getting", "what it costs") are informational paraphrases — not the provider's official wording.

Privacy

QUE is a static, account-free widget. It doesn't collect personal information like your name, email, or location. It does use your browser's local storage to remember which offer you're on and which offers you've already explored, so refreshing the page doesn't lose your place or double-count a click.

Limits of this information

Everything on this page and every offer card is provided for general information only. QUE doesn't guarantee that any offer, price, or detail shown is current, complete, or error-free. When something here and a provider's own site disagree, the provider's site is correct.
