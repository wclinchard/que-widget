How QUE works

What QUE is

QUE is a simple way to browse offers — subscriptions, tools, services — one at a time, instead of a ranked list or a wall of logos competing for attention. The goal is to let you read what an offer actually includes before you know whose it is.

Why the offer comes before the brand

A familiar logo can make the same offer feel more trustworthy, and an unfamiliar one can make it feel riskier, regardless of what's actually being offered. QUE hides the provider name at first so "what you're getting" and "what it costs" can be read on their own terms. Nothing is permanently hidden — you can reveal the brand any time.

Categories

Offers are grouped into categories (AI, Music, Software, and so on). Choosing a category only limits which pool of offers can appear — it doesn't rank or prioritize anything within that pool. Categories with no offers yet simply don't show up in the filter.

How offers are chosen

Within a category, QUE uses a "shuffle-bag": every offer is shown once, in a random order, before any offer repeats. Once the whole pool has been shown, it reshuffles into a new random order for the next cycle, and the last offer of one cycle is never immediately followed by itself in the next. This process doesn't rank, score, or prioritize any offer over another — it's random order, not a leaderboard.

Can payment affect placement?

No. Which offer appears, and in what order, is decided only by the shuffle-bag described above. QUE doesn't currently have any paid placement, sponsorship, or affiliate relationships — and if that ever changes, it'll be disclosed in this panel. A commercial relationship is never allowed to secretly influence which offer shows up or when.

Where offers come from

Offers are currently curated and entered manually, based on each provider's own publicly available pricing and product pages — there's no automated feed yet. Existing offers are reviewed and updated as pricing or features change, but on no fixed schedule, so the provider's own site is always the most current source. See "Requests & corrections" below to suggest an offer or flag one that's out of date.

What "have explored" means

Each offer shows how many times "Learn more" has been clicked for it. This is browser-based tracking for the current MVP, not verified unique-person data — it reflects clicks a browser has recorded, not confirmed individual people, and it resets like any other locally stored browser data if that storage is cleared.

Clicking "Learn more"

Clicking "Learn more" opens the provider's own official website in a new tab, and — once per offer, per browser — counts toward that offer's exploration number. QUE isn't affiliated with, endorsed by, or representing any listed provider unless explicitly stated.

Accuracy & updates

Pricing, features, limits, and availability change, and the information shown here can fall out of date. Always confirm details on the provider's own site before deciding anything. The summaries on each card ("what you're getting", "what it costs") are informational paraphrases — not the provider's official wording.

Privacy

QUE is a static, account-free widget. It doesn't collect personal information like your name, email, or location. It does use your browser's local storage to remember which offer you're on and which offers you've already explored, so refreshing the page doesn't lose your place or double-count a click.

Limits of this information

Everything on this page and every offer card is provided for general information only. QUE doesn't guarantee that any offer, price, or detail shown is current, complete, or error-free. When something here and a provider's own site disagree, the provider's site is correct.
